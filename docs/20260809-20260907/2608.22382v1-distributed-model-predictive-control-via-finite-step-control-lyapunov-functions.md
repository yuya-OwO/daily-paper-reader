## 摘要
与经典逆Lyapunov定理不同，有限步逆结论具有构造性并提供了不同的出发点：对于足够大的有限前视步长（记为M），在明确意义下，任何缩放范数都可作为逆有限步Lyapunov函数。对于互联离散时间系统，类似的论证思路可导出“非保守”的小增益条件。受此观点启发，本文针对受约束的互联非线性离散时间系统提出了一种分布式模型预测控制框架。每个子系统的每个时间步仅求解一个局部优化问题，其局部阶段函数设置为局部控制有限步型Lyapunov函数形式，并利用时间对齐的邻居预测、优化的状态约束收紧半径以及有限步小增益终端不等式。由于接收到的邻居预测未必等于未来滚动时域优化生成的轨迹，名义逆证书本身不足以保证递推可行性与稳定性。为此，我们发展了移位兼容约束裕度、针对仿射控制网络的局部一步终端可行性检验，以及预测与再优化失配的解析界。所得分析给出了递推可行性、约束满足性以及实用的M步Lyapunov估计；当预测与再优化失配界趋于零时，可实现渐近收敛。对于受约束的线性网络，相关条件可归结为有限维矩阵、QP及SOCP检验。该框架被专门应用于两台直流发电机（DGU）直流微电网中DC/DC功率变换器运行约束下的电流共享与终端母线电压安全，并在小型实验室样机上进行了评估。

## Abstract
As opposed to classical converse Lyapunov theorems, finite-step converse results are constructive and offer a different starting point: for sufficiently large finite step ahead, say $M$, in an explicit sense, any scaled norm can serve as a converse finite-step Lyapunov function. As for interconnected discrete-time systems, similar lines of argument lead to ``non-conservative'' small-gain conditions. Motivated by this viewpoint, this paper develops a distributed model predictive control framework for constrained interconnected nonlinear discrete-time systems. Each subsystem solves one local optimization problem at each system time step instant by setting the local stage function in form of a local control finite-step like Lyapunov function, using time-aligned neighbor predictions, optimized state-constraint tightening radii, and a finite-step small-gain terminal inequality. Since received neighbor predictions need not equal the trajectories generated by future receding-horizon optimizations, the nominal converse certificates do not alone ensure recursive feasibility or stability. We therefore develop shift-compatible constraint margins, a local one-step terminal feasibility test for networks that are affine in control, and an analytical bound for the prediction and reoptimization mismatch. The resulting analysis gives recursive feasibility, constraint satisfaction, and a practical $M$-step Lyapunov estimate, with asymptotic convergence when the prediction and reoptimization mismatch bound tends to zero. For constrained linear networks, the conditions reduce to finite-dimensional matrix, QP, and SOCP tests. The framework is specialized to current sharing and terminal bus voltage safety under DC/DC power converters' operational constraints in a two-DGU DC microgrid evaluated on a small laboratory-scale prototype.
