## 摘要
有理稳定性是非线性常微分方程（ODE）的一种定量稳定性概念，在该概念下，解的收敛性由随时间有理衰减的界来刻画。尽管已有有理稳定性的 Lyapunov 刻画被提出，但现有条件尚未被证明能够精确刻画有理速率性能。本文通过给出具有指定速率性能的区域有理稳定性的精确逆 Lyapunov 刻画，解决了这一问题。为了对所得到的逆 Lyapunov 条件进行数值检验，提出了一组分层的凸松弛，这些凸松弛可利用平方和（SOS）规划来施加。每一层都与一个关于速率性能的保守性缩放因子的猜想相关联，并进行了数值验证。所提出的第一层似乎是新颖的，并表明其性能优于经典的基于 SOS 的方法。数值例子用于验证结果，并计算状态空间中的嵌套区域，在这些区域上可以保证指定的性能水平。

## Abstract
Rational stability is a quantitative stability notion for nonlinear ordinary differential equations (ODEs), under which convergence is characterized by a rationally-in-time decaying bound on solutions. Although Lyapunov characterizations of rational stability have been proposed, no existing condition has been shown to exactly characterize rational rate performance. The paper resolves this issue by providing an exact converse Lyapunov characterization of regional rational stability with prescribed rate performance. To test the resulting converse Lyapunov conditions numerically, a tiered set of convex relaxations is proposed which can be enforced using Sum-of-Squares (SOS) programming. A conjectured conservatism scaling factor in rate performance is associated with each tier and validated numerically. The first proposed tier appears to be novel and is shown to outperform classical SOS-based approaches. Numerical examples are used to validate the results and compute nested regions of state-space on which prescribed levels of performance can be guaranteed.
