## 摘要
我们研究具有有界输出算子的 Banach 空间中线性无穷维系统的指数输出-状态稳定性（eOSS）。结果表明，eOSS 等价于存在蕴含形式的强制 eOSS Lyapunov 函数。此外，指数可检测性保证了耗散形式的强制 eOSS Lyapunov 函数的存在，从而保证了 eOSS。反例表明，一般情况下逆向蕴含不成立：指数零可检测性不蕴含 eOSS，eOSS 不蕴含耗散形式的 eOSS Lyapunov 函数的存在，而后者反过来又不蕴含指数可检测性。如果不稳定子空间是有限维的，则零可检测性蕴含指数可检测性，并给出 eOSS 的等价刻画。所得结果通过一个抛物型方程加以说明。

## Abstract
We study exponential output-to-state stability (eOSS) of linear infinite-dimensional systems in Banach spaces with bounded output operators. It is shown that eOSS is equivalent to the existence of a coercive eOSS Lyapunov function in implication form. Also exponential detectability guarantees the existence of a coercive eOSS Lyapunov function in dissipation form and therefore eOSS. Counterexamples demonstrate that the converse implications fail in general: exponential zero-detectability does not imply eOSS, eOSS does not imply the existence of an eOSS Lyapunov function in dissipation form, which, in turn, does not imply exponential detectability. If the unstable subspace is finite-dimensional, zero-detectability implies exponential detectability and yields equivalent characterizations of eOSS. The results are illustrated with a parabolic equation.
