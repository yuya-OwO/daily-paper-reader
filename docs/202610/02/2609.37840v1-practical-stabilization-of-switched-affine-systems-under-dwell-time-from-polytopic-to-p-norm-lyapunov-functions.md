## 摘要
本文研究在驻留时间约束下，切换仿射系统围绕一个并非各子系统公共平衡点的期望工作点的镇定问题。由于在此情形下渐近镇定通常无法实现，目标是确保轨迹收敛到并保持在包含期望工作点的给定区域内。主要贡献是发展了基于 Lyapunov 的工具，使其能够处理比二次 Lyapunov 函数所诱导的目标区域更一般的目标区域。特别地，我们为一大类加权 p-范数函数建立了构造性 Lyapunov 刻画，该刻画推广了经典的二次 Lyapunov 等价性，并支撑了所提出的混合控制与驻留时间方面的进展。针对期望区域为多面体的情形，并在现有基于 Lyapunov 的方法基础上，我们提出一种构造性的两步方法：从平均系统的多面体 Lyapunov 证书出发，得到对所有充分大的 p 均适用的光滑加权 p-范数 Lyapunov 函数。我们证明，这一扩展保证了期望工作点的实用渐近稳定性，并在最小驻留时间切换下给出了经 Lyapunov 验证的不变区域。数值例子说明了所提方法的有效性。

## Abstract
This paper addresses the stabilization of switched affine systems under dwell-time constraints around a desired operating point that is not a common equilibrium of the subsystems. Since asymptotic stabilization is generally unattainable in this setting, the objective is to ensure that trajectories converge to and remain within a prescribed region containing the desired operating point. The main contribution is the development of Lyapunov-based tools that accommodate target regions more general than those induced by quadratic Lyapunov functions. In particular, we establish a constructive Lyapunov characterization for a broad family of weighted $p$-norm functions, which generalizes the classical quadratic Lyapunov equivalence and underpins the proposed hybrid-control and dwell-time developments. Focusing on the case in which the desired region is a polytope, and building on existing Lyapunov-based methods, we develop a constructive two-step method that starts from a polytopic Lyapunov certificate for the average system and yields a smooth weighted $p$-norm Lyapunov function for all sufficiently large $p$. We prove that this extension guarantees practical asymptotic stability of the desired operating point and yields a Lyapunov-certified invariant region under minimum dwell-time switching. Numerical examples illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
