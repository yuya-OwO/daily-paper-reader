## 摘要
动力系统的收缩分析将一个全局收敛问题转化为局部问题。如果变分系统在一致意义下稳定，那么原系统的所有轨迹都会相互收敛。本文为连续时间随机系统发展了随机对应理论，其中收敛性由概率律之间的 Wasserstein 距离来度量。关键步骤是利用均方微分来构造随机变分动力学。其一致 $L_2$ 稳定性便可沿初始条件曲线积分，从而得到相应概率律的 Wasserstein 收缩。由此得到以微分 Lyapunov 函数表述的判据，而在流形上，它导向内蕴的黎曼表述。随机情形的一个显著特征是噪声的耦合成为一种设计自由度。为了利用这一自由度，我们引入一种正交微分耦合，将框架扩展到同步耦合之外。所得判据明确揭示了状态空间的曲率如何进入收缩机制，并且在正交标架情形下恢复了经典的 Ricci 曲率结构。最后，我们将该框架应用于紧流形上的随机控制。通过引入随机控制输入，我们克服了全局镇定中的一个确定性障碍。也就是说，一个无法通过光滑时不变确定性反馈实现全局镇定的系统，被使得在 $W_2$ 中全局收缩，并且其稳态分布可以被任意地集中在某个预先给定的目标平衡点周围。

## Abstract
Contraction analysis of dynamical systems turns a global convergence problem into a local one. If the variational system is stable in a uniform sense, then all trajectories of the original system converge toward each other. This paper develops a stochastic analogue for continuous-time stochastic systems, where convergence is measured by Wasserstein distance between probability laws. The key step is to employ mean-square differentiation to construct stochastic variational dynamics. Its uniform $L_2$ stability can then be integrated along curves of initial conditions, yielding Wasserstein contraction of the associated probability laws. This yields criteria formulated in terms of differential Lyapunov functions, and on manifolds, it leads to intrinsic Riemannian formulations. A distinctive feature of the stochastic setting is that the coupling of the noises becomes a design freedom. To exploit this freedom, we introduce an orthogonal differential coupling that extends the framework beyond synchronous coupling. The resulting criterion explicitly exposes how the curvature of the state space enters the contraction mechanism and, in the orthonormal-frame case, recovers the classical Ricci-curvature structure. Finally, we apply the framework to stochastic control on a compact manifold. By introducing stochastic control input, we overcome a deterministic obstruction to global stabilization. That is, a system that cannot be globally stabilized through smooth time-invariant deterministic feedback is rendered globally contracting in $W_2$, and its steady state distribution can be made arbitrarily concentrated around a prescribed target equilibrium.
