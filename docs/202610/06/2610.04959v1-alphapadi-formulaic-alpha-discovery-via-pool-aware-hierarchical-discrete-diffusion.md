## 摘要
公式化 Alpha 发现旨在寻找能够预测横截面资产收益的符号表达式。在部署中，多个公式被组合成一个 Alpha 池，其中每个公式的价值取决于其对联合预测性能所贡献的互补信息。尽管强化学习和生成流网络已成为生成公式化 Alpha 的有前景范式，但现有框架面临三个相互关联的挑战。首先，逐个生成公式使池上下文和公式间互补性处于生成状态之外。其次，按公式生成缺乏统一机制来在不同层级上保留和修正结构。第三，池级奖励共同反映预测性能和冗余度，但无法通过符号评估直接进行微分以训练生成器。为克服这些挑战，我们提出 AlphaPADI（通过池感知分层离散扩散的公式化 Alpha 发现），这是一个围绕三个组件构建的新框架：（1）语法约束的缓冲区初始化，用于构造语法有效的池候选；（2）池感知分层扩散，在当前池上下文下于多个结构尺度上重建完整池；（3）奖励引导的池精炼，评估联合预测性能和内部多样性，更新精英缓冲区，并通过重建和偏好学习训练反向模型。在中国和美国股票市场上的实证结果表明，AlphaPADI 在预测性能和投资组合性能上均优于所评估的基线，从而验证了池感知生成是一种有效的自动化 Alpha 发现框架。

## Abstract
Formulaic alpha discovery seeks symbolic expressions that predict cross-sectional asset returns. In deployment, multiple formulas are combined into an alpha pool, where each formula is valued through the complementary information it contributes to joint predictive performance. While Reinforcement Learning and Generative Flow Networks have emerged as promising paradigms for generating formulaic alphas, existing frameworks face three related challenges. First, generating formulas individually leaves pool context and inter-formula complementarity outside the generative state. Second, formula-wise generation lacks a unified mechanism for preserving and revising structures at different levels. Third, pool-level rewards jointly reflect predictive performance and redundancy but cannot be differentiated directly through symbolic evaluation to train the generator. To overcome these challenges, we introduce AlphaPADI (Formulaic Alpha Discovery via Pool-Aware Hierarchical Discrete Diffusion), a novel framework built around three components: (1) grammar-constrained buffer initialization that constructs syntactically valid pool candidates, (2) pool-aware hierarchical diffusion that reconstructs complete pools at multiple structural scales under the current pool context, and (3) reward-guided pool refinement that evaluates joint predictive performance and inner diversity, updates the elite buffer, and trains the reverse model through reconstruction and preference learning. Empirical results on the Chinese and U.S. stock markets demonstrate that AlphaPADI outperforms the evaluated baselines in both predictive and portfolio performance, thereby validating pool-aware generation as an effective framework for automated alpha discovery.
